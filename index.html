What we make

One thing, made fresh

We are a small family shop with one thing on the board. Zoran Alimpić learned the recipe from his wife Dušanka, whose family brought it from Serbia. It has not changed much: whole McIntosh apples, batter, hot oil, cinnamon sugar.

  1. 1

    Peeled, cored, cut

    An apple goes onto the machine behind the counter and comes off as even rings — no pre-made dough, no frozen discs.

  2. 2

    Battered and fried

    The rings are dipped in batter and lowered into the fryer. A few minutes later they come out golden and are dusted with cinnamon sugar.

  3. 3

    Eaten hot, in the nook

    Plain in a box, or as the Lake Special: three warm fritters, a scoop of vanilla ice cream, and maple, caramel or chocolate syrup.

Our story

Opened by a father, on his wife’s recipe

Zoran Alimpić came to Canada from Serbia as a young man with a couple of suitcases. Apple fritters were the comfort food at home — his daughter Zorana grew up with friends coming over for them — and his wife Dušanka kept the recipe. They moved to Niagara about five years before the shop opened, and in June 2021 Fritters on the Lake started frying on King Street.

Dušanka’s health means she is not at the counter all day, but she stops by from time to time to check that the fritters are up to standard. Zorana lives in South Africa with her own family and came back for the summers to help; much of what appears on our Instagram is written by her.

The shop drew a crowd within days of opening. Niagara This Week sent reporter Luke Edwards to write about it, and the Shaw Festival company stopped by early on. Neighbours brought flowers, and one brought a pizza during the renovation. Gift cards are available at the counter.

Questions we get asked

Are the apple fritters nut-free?

Yes — the fritters themselves are nut-allergy friendly. We buy in the syrups and the ice cream, so we cannot recommend those.

Are the fritters vegan?

Unfortunately not. We use both eggs and milk in the batter.

Can we pre-order?

For two dozen or more, yes: call between Monday and Wednesday, for collection before 4:00 pm, with a day’s notice. Smaller orders are first come, first fried.

Do you deliver?

Yes — the order link sends you to Uber Eats, where you can choose pickup or delivery and see what is available at your address.
